About KJSKAI

KJSKAI promotes authentic Japanese Shotokan Karate and traditional Kobudo training in India with emphasis on discipline, respect, character development, technical excellence and traditional dojo values.

Chief Instructor

Kyoshi Mohammad Jahangir

• 7th Dan Black Belt (Japan Diploma)

• Awarded 7th Dan by Kancho Masaya Kohama (9th Dan ISSKA Japan)
• Technical guidance and training with Shihan Tamang Pemba (9th Dan NSKF Japan)
• Traditional Kobudo training under Dr. A.S. Kobudo Krishnamurti

Lineage & Technical Heritage

Kiyo Japan Shotokan Karate Association India (KJSKAI) is dedicated to preserving and promoting traditional Japanese Shotokan Karate and Kobudo. Under the leadership of Kyoshi Mohammad Jahangir, KJSKAI maintains authentic dojo traditions, discipline, technical excellence and character development. Kyoshi Mohammad Jahangir received his 7th Dan Black Belt from Kancho Masaya Kohama (9th Dan ISSKA Japan) and continues technical development and practice with Shihan Tamang Pemba (9th Dan NSKF Japan). Through these influences, KJSKAI strives to preserve authentic martial arts heritage while developing future generations of karate practitioners.

Traditional Kobudo

KJSKAI also promotes traditional Kobudo training. Kobudo focuses on the study and preservation of classical Okinawan weapons and traditional martial arts methods. Kyoshi Mohammad Jahangir received Kobudo instruction under Dr. A.S. Kobudo Krishnamurti and continues to support the preservation of traditional martial arts culture.
